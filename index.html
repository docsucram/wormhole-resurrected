🛸 FLIGHT & PROPULSION

[W] / [▲ UP]: Main Thrusters. Accelerates your ship forward. Generates a plasma exhaust trail.

[A] / [D] or [◀ / ▶]: Agility Rotation. Steers ship orientation instantly.

RETROS (BRAKING): When equipped with Retros, easing off the thruster applies automatic reverse damping to stop drifting.

⚔ WEAPONS & COMBAT SYSTEMS

[SPACE]: Pulse Cannons. Upgradable from single center fire to dual-cannons and wide-angle triple spread.

[F]: Secondary Powerup Launch. Fires the topmost stored hazard projectile forward into enemy wormholes.

[R] / [SHIFT]: Class Special Ability. Activates unique ship features (Turtle Cannon, ShapeShift, HeatSeeker array, Attractor/Repulser field).

[ESC]: Tactical Pause. Opens the in-flight pause menu.

🌀 ORBITAL WORMHOLE MECHANICS

Each pilot commands an orbital wormhole tethered to their realm. Wormholes continuously revolve along the outer orbit.

⚡ EXTRACTING POWERUPS

Continually shoot opponent wormholes with your pulse cannons. Delivering sustained damage destabilizes their event horizon, ejecting fresh powerup capsules into the arena!

🚀 TRANSMITTING HAZARDS

Collect offensive powerups, line up your sights, and press [F] to launch the projectile into the opponent's wormhole. Once absorbed, the hazard warps directly into their arena to attack them!

GRAVITATIONAL VORTEX: Projectiles and powerup capsules entering near a wormhole are naturally pulled into the singularity by its gravitational field.

GHOST-PUD PUNTING: Wandering Ghost-Puds can be shot and punted across the arena with your lasers. Knocking them into an opponent's wormhole warps them to that opponent!
