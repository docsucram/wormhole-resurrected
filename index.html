OVERVIEW

Wormhole Resurrected is a fast paced arcade action game. In this game, you have the chance to select your ship and fly for survival against other internet players. You collect powerups to send at your opponents, while dodging the enemy powerups your opponents are sending at you. You can also destroy enemies sent to your screen with your pulse cannons as well as shoot wormholes to generate more powerups. The last ship or team standing wins.

The game is based on Wormhole originally released by Centerfleet (later Centerscore). The game also showed up on Newgrounds after becoming popular. Sadly, on December 31 2002, the game and website were shutdown.

GETTING & SENDING POWERUPS

Destroy enemies or continually shoot an opponent’s wormholes to generate powerups.

Getting powerups is a crucial part of the game. Pilot your ship to collect as many powerups as you can. Each powerup will either upgrade your ship, clear the screen of enemies, or give you an enemy to send at your opponents.

Once you have collected an Offensive Powerup you can fire it at an opponent's wormhole. If the powerup hits the wormhole, the powerup transforms into an enemy on your opponent's screen.

INVENTORY CAPACITY: You can hold up to 5 Offensive powerups at once.

FLIGHT & PROPULSION

[W] / [▲ UP]: Main Thrusters. Accelerates your ship forward. Generates a plasma exhaust trail.

[A] / [D] or [◀ / ▶]: Agility Rotation. Steers ship orientation instantly.

RETROS (BRAKING): When equipped with Retros, easing off the thruster applies automatic reverse damping to stop drifting.

WEAPONS & COMBAT SYSTEMS

[SPACE]: Pulse Cannons. Upgradable from single center fire to dual-cannons and wide-angle triple spread.

[F]: Secondary Powerup Launch. Fires the topmost stored hazard projectile forward into enemy wormholes.

[R] / [SHIFT]: Class Special Ability. Activates unique ship features (Turtle Cannon, ShapeShift, HeatSeeker array, Attractor/Repulser field).

[ESC]: System Menu. Opens the in-flight system & match menu.

XBOX & CONTROLLER SUPPORT

LEFT STICK / D-PAD: Steer ship orientation.

RIGHT TRIGGER [RT] / BUTTON [X]: Main Thrusters (Analog acceleration).

LEFT TRIGGER [LT] / BUTTON [A]: Primary Pulse Cannons (Fire / Menu Select).

LEFT BUMPER [LB] / BUTTON [B]: Launch Stored Powerup (Back / Cancel).

RIGHT BUMPER [RB] / BUTTON [Y]: Ship Special Ability.

START / MENU BUTTON: System Menu // Pause.

OPENING GAMEPLAY

Immediately target the opponent's wormhole with primary lasers to spawn powerups right away. Upgraded guns eject powerups even faster.

INVENTORY CAPACITY

Your fighter holds a maximum of 5 stored hazards in the top tray. Shoot them into wormholes using [F] promptly to overwhelm your opponent.

COUNTERMEASURES

Save ZAP attacks and Invulnerability Shields when opponents send Nukes, Sweeping Beams, or massive HeatSeeker swarms.

THERMONUCLEAR NUKE EVASION

When a Nuke detonates, its blast wave expands outward as an expanding shockwave ring. You can avoid all damage by positioning your ship directly in the center of the detonation right when it explodes!

PUNTING GHOST-PUDS

Use your lasers to push wandering Ghost-Puds into enemy wormholes to turn an indestructible hazard against your opponent!
