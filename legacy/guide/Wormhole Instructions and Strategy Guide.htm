Wormhole - Getting Powerups
Getting powerups is a crucial part of the game. Pilot your ship to collect as many powerups as you can. Each powerup will either upgrade your ship, clear the screen of enemies, or give you an enemy to send at your opponents. For the complete rundown on powerups see Offense Powerups and Defense Powerups.

In the image to the right, the pilot is chasing down a Nuke powerup.
Powerups appear when you destroy an enemy or when you continually shoot an opponent's wormhole. Its recommended at the beginning of the game to start shooting wormholes right away to generate powerups to pickup.

In the image to the left, a pilot generated a nuke powerup and an upgrade powerup by shooting an opponent's wormhole.

The more powerful your pulse cannons, the faster you can generate powerups from a wormhole.
