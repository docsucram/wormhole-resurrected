Wormhole - Defensive Powerups

There are two main types of powerups - offensive powerups and defensive powerups.

Defensive Powerups

Defensive powerups are used to help upgrade your ship, clear your screen of all enemies, or generate a brief shield of invulnerability.

The upgrade powerup will either upgrade your ship's weapon power or will upgrade your ship's speed or gives you retros which act as automatic brakes.

The invulnerability powerup will create a shield of invincibility around your ship for a few seconds. This shield will even stop a nuclear blast!

The clear screen powerup will clear all enemies on your visible screen, including nukes and GHOST PUDS!

The turret is the ultimate defense unit. Fire these into a wormhole and they will circle that wormhole, protecting it by firing rapid cannons at your opponent!
