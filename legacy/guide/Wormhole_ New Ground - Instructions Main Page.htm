Wormhole: New Ground

Wormhole: New Ground is a fast paced arcade action game. In this game, you have the chance to select your ship and fly for survival against other internet players. You collect powerups to send at your opponents, while dodging the enemy powerups your opponents are sending at you. You can also destroy enemies sent to your screen with your pulse cannons as well as shoot wormholes to generate more powerups. The last ship or team standing wins.
   
As a multi-player internet game, you can chat with potential opponents before entering a game as well as discuss helpful hints and strategies with other players. Configure your game options and battle as a team or in a free for all with players from around the world. With experience you'll develop your own unique play style.

New Ground is a fully immersive service as players are assigned missions based on player records and statistics. Rise and fall in rank with each successive win or loss. Can you become number 1?
   
Please read through the following instructions to familiarize yourself with the game interface and features. The first section will run over all the essential elements from chatting in the lobby to confirguring and starting your own game.
