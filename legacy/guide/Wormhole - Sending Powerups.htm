Wormhole - Sending Powerups
Once you have collected an enemy powerup you can fire it at an opponent's wormhole. If the powerup hits the wormhole, the powerup transforms into an enemy on your opponent's screen!

Firing the powerup into an enemy's wormhole also serves the purpose of helping to generate more, new powerups faster!

Remember you use the "f" key to fire the powerup.
You can also use enemy powerups as powerful weapons that will destroy most enemies in one shot. Enemies destroyed by powerup shots will explode into fragments that may destroy nearby enemies as well.

The best players will often sacrifice their powerups to shoot a barrage of incoming enemies to destroy them all with one shot. After all, the loss of one enemy powerup is better than a large loss of health!
