Wormhole - Offensive Powerups

Offensive powerups appear in your powerup bin at the top of the screen and can be sent at opponent's wormholes to create enemies on their screens. You can hold a maximum of five powerups at once. Any additional powerups will be lost, so once you collect five, shoot them as fast as possible.

The inflator powerup sends large inflators that continually expand until they split upon colliding with a ship or are destroyed.

The mine powerup creates a starbust of mines around your enemy's wormhole.


The mine layer powerup creates a duo of mine layers that zig-zag their way across the screen laying down mines until destroyed.

The wallcrawler powerup sends enemies that crawl up and down the walls, shooting at a ship when it is in sight.

The scarab powerup sends a pair of enemies that grab powerups from the screen and use them against the ship on the screen!

The gunship powerup sends a wandering ship that shoots any ship in sight with its rotating turrets.

The UFO powerup sends a trio of kamikaze UFOs that follow aship until they are shot down.

The heatseekers powerup sends a horde of heatseekers which will follow a ship until they detonate either on the ship or on a wall.

The nuke powerup sends a lethal blast across most of the game board. The closer a ship is to the nuke during the blast, the more damage taken!

The EMP powerup is a homing bomb that disrupts your ship's systems. Ships caught in an EMP shockwave will have their flight controls scrambled for a short amount of time.

The ultimate beam is a super cannon discharged from a wormhole. The beam itself has an awesome range and locks onto a target and follows the acquired target for a brief amount of time. Fleeing is advised.

All the PUDS your smashed in Avalanche are back to haunt you. Send two ghost puds with with each powerup. Ghost puds are immune to damage and will randomly wander your screen. Shoot them back into wormholes for primo annoyance.
